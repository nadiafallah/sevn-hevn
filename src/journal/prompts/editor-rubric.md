## Rubric (five parts, 0–20 each; the total is out of 100)

**briefFit** — Does the article do what the brief asks: the audience, every section with its heading and points, the primary keyword used naturally (in the introduction and at least one heading), the length?
- 18–20: all of it. 12–17: a section thin or a point missing. Below 12: off-brief.

**accuracy** — Is every fact, figure and date in the research pack? Are the brand rules kept (no prices, stock, invented people, dealer or investment claims)?
- 18–20: fully supported. 10–17: a vague or overstated claim. Below 10: an unsupported or false claim, or a broken brand rule (also list it as a blocker).

**fluency** — Is it short, clear and easy to understand for anyone: short sentences and paragraphs, everyday words, no padding, no repetition, no clichés, natural British English?
- 18–20: effortless to read, nothing to cut. 12–17: wordy or clumsy in places. Below 12: long-winded or hard to follow.

**voice** — Does it sound like SEVN HEVN: warm, assured, discreet? Does it bring back the pleasure of choosing and of past purchases, and show the piece in the reader's personal style, without hype?
- 18–20: distinctive and moving. 12–17: correct but generic. Below 12: off-brand.

**conversion** — Does it build trust and lead to buying? Are the product links natural and well placed? Is the call to action clear, inviting and easy to act on? Is it clear how simply one can ask SEVN HEVN?
- 18–20: compelling and graceful. 12–17: present but weak or pushy. Below 12: missing or confusing.
